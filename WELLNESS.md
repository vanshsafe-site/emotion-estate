## Care and safety
- Immediate help button with click-to-call emergency services for India, the United States, the UK & Ireland, and the European Union, plus crisis support contacts and an international helpline directory.
- Self-guided wellbeing toolkit with progressive muscle relaxation, grounding, a brief stress reset, a wind-down routine, and self-compassion prompts.
- Existing breathing, journaling, ambient sound, and 5–4–3–2–1 grounding exercises remain available.
- Self-guided exercises are optional general wellbeing practices, not diagnosis, treatment, or a substitute for professional or emergency support. Emergency numbers vary by location; use the local number where you are.
